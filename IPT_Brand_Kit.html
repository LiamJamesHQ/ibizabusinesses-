🏷️ Logo

Every Ibiza Party Transfers document carries the logo. Three assets, each with one job.

Transparent PNGThe default. Always attempted first, on every ground.

Rules

  • ✅ Transparent first. The transparent PNG is used on heroes, dark bands and photographs. The solid JPG only appears where its square edge disappears, never in a hero.
  • 🌙 The logo is white lettering, so it lives on dark grounds: the hero, the navy footer band, the purple veil. It never sits on white or lavender.
  • 📐 Clear space around the logo equals the height of the letter i on every side. Minimum width on screen is 120px.
  • 🚫 Never stretch, recolour, rotate, or add a drop shadow.
  • 📱 QR code goes at the foot of every document next to the logo, inside the navy footer band.
  • 🔗 Open Graph image on every shareable page is the solid JPG, so the WhatsApp preview shows a filled square.

😀 Emoji

Emoji are the icon system. No SVG icons, no icon fonts. Every heading, pill, feature card and list item leads with one.

  • 🚐 The transfer set: 🚐 ✈️ 🥂 📍 🏁 📅 ⏰ 👥 🧳 💬 💡 🎵 💶. Reach for these before anything else.
  • 💜 The purple heart is the only heart used on Ibiza Party Transfers.
  • 🎵 Use 🎵 not 🎶, and 📱 not 📲. Both alternatives render badly.
  • 🚫 Banned because they render in monochrome: ⚽ 🏟 🎧 🖤 🏝 🍴
  • 🧪 A new emoji must pass the colour test before use: rendered through Playwright, more than 60 pixels with a channel spread above 40 confirms it is in colour.
  • 1️⃣ One emoji per line. It goes first, then a space, then the text. Never two in a row.

💬 Voice

Casual, warm, British. Transfers serve hens and stags alike, so the register follows who is reading.

Address them as lovely. Close with x.

💙 Boys

Address them as lads. Close with cheers.

Alright lads, your party bus is booked for Friday. Here is everything you need for the airport. Cheers

Rules for both

  • 🔠 Normal sentence case. A capital at the start of every sentence and on names and places. Lowercase only is wrong.
  • 📖 Literal language only. No idioms, analogies or figures of speech. Say exactly what is booked, when, where, and what it costs.
  • ✂️ Short sentences. One idea each.
  • 🚫 No em dashes or en dashes. Use a full stop or a comma instead.
  • 🙂 Warm, not gushing. No exclamation marks in a row, no cheerleading.
  • 🎀 Every transfer document mentions Pretty Intricate once, in the highlight card or the sign-off, because the transfer is usually the start of a bigger trip.

📐 Layout and build

How the page is put together, and the technical rules that keep it rendering correctly everywhere.

  • 🃏 One white card on a lavender page, maximum width 900px, centred with margin: 0 auto, 24px corner radius, overflow hidden so the hero and footer band bleed to the edges.
  • ↔️ Spacing: 48px side padding inside the card, 36px between sections, 20px between cards in a grid. On phones, 22px sides and single columns.
  • 🖼️ Photographs are always veiled in purple and never boxed with a plain border. Hero height 480px on desktop, 440px on mobile. The hero ends on a hard edge, no dissolve.
  • 🌙 Dark bands are allowed between white sections for feature cards and calls to action. Never two in a row.
  • 🔘 Every button and pill is fully rounded. This is the one place the documents depart from the website.
  • 📄 DOCTYPE first, then <meta charset="UTF-8"> as the first line in the head, or emoji render as garbled symbols.
  • 🔗 Open Graph tags on every shareable page so WhatsApp shows a preview card with the solid logo.
  • 🧷 Images are base64 embedded when a PDF or PNG is rendered through Playwright, because the CDN blocks it. CDN links are fine on live pages.
  • 📁 Files for GitHub Pages end in .html, never .htm. Use top/right/bottom/left: 0 rather than inset: 0 for Shopify.
  • 🚫 No italics and no black. Both are disabled in the stylesheet.
