This is the reference for every Pretty Intricate asset: quotes, booking confirmations, product pages, brochures, reminders. Each rule below is shown live, so the document doubles as a working example. Build from this, not from the old templates.

🏷️ Logo

Every Pretty Intricate document carries the logo. There are three assets and each has one job.

Transparent PNGThe default. Always attempted first, on every ground.
Solid JPGFallback only. Plain white grounds, where its square edge disappears.
QR codeFoot of every document, beside the logo.

Rules

  • ✅ Transparent first. The transparent PNG is used on heroes, coloured grounds and photographs. The solid JPG only appears on plain white, never in a hero, because its white square shows.
  • ☀️ On a hero the logo sits top left over the lightest part of the image, usually the sky. Never over the dark veil at the bottom, where it cannot be read.
  • 📐 Clear space around the logo equals the height of the pink triangle on every side. Minimum width on screen is 120px.
  • 🚫 Never stretch, recolour, rotate, crop the triangle, or add a drop shadow.
  • 📱 QR code goes at the foot of every document next to the logo, on white or blush only.

Rules

  • 🚫 No italics anywhere. The italic style is disabled in every file.
  • 🔠 Headings are title case. Body copy is sentence case with a capital at the start of every sentence and on names and places.
  • 📏 Body lines run no longer than about 72 characters. Wider than that, add a column.
  • 🔤 Fallbacks are Georgia for Lora and Segoe UI or Arial for Source Sans 3, so nothing breaks if the fonts fail to load.

😀 Emoji

Emoji are the icon system. No SVG icons, no icon fonts. Every heading, pill and list item leads with one.

  • 💜 The purple heart is the only heart used on Pretty Intricate.
  • 🎵 Use 🎵 not 🎶, and 📱 not 📲. Both alternatives render badly.
  • 🚫 Banned because they render in monochrome: ⚽ 🏟 🎧 🖤 🏝 🍴
  • 🧪 A new emoji must pass the colour test before use: rendered through Playwright, more than 60 pixels with a channel spread above 40 confirms it is in colour.
  • 1️⃣ One emoji per line. It goes first, then a space, then the text. Never two in a row, never one at the end of a sentence except the 💜 after a name.

💬 Voice

Casual, warm, British. Two registers depending on who is reading.

💜 Girls

Address them as lovely. Close with x.

Hi lovely, your cheeky butler is locked in for Saturday. Here is everything you need. x

💙 Boys

Address them as lads. Close with cheers.

Alright lads, your boat is booked for Saturday. Here is everything you need. Cheers

Rules for both

  • 🔠 Normal sentence case. A capital at the start of every sentence and on names and places. Lowercase only is wrong.
  • 📖 Literal language only. No idioms, analogies or figures of speech. Say exactly what is booked, when, where, and what it costs.
  • ✂️ Short sentences. One idea each.
  • 🚫 No em dashes or en dashes. Use a full stop or a comma instead.
  • 🙂 Warm, not gushing. No exclamation marks in a row, no cheerleading.

📐 Layout and build

How the page is put together, and the technical rules that keep it rendering correctly everywhere.

  • 🃏 One white card on a blush page, maximum width 900px, centred with margin: 0 auto, 24px corner radius, overflow hidden so the hero bleeds to the edges.
  • ↔️ Spacing: 48px side padding inside the card, 36px between sections, 20px between cards in a grid. On phones, 22px sides and single columns.
  • 🖼️ Photographs are full-bleed or washed, never boxed with a border. Hero height 460px on desktop, 360px on mobile.
  • 📄 DOCTYPE first, then <meta charset="UTF-8"> as the first line in the head, or emoji render as garbled symbols.
  • 🔗 Open Graph tags on every shareable page so WhatsApp shows a preview card with the logo.
  • 🧷 Images are base64 embedded when a PDF or PNG is rendered through Playwright, because the CDN blocks it. CDN links are fine on live Shopify pages.
  • 📁 Files for GitHub Pages end in .html, never .htm. Use top/right/bottom/left: 0 rather than inset: 0 for Shopify.
  • 🚫 No italics and no black. Both are disabled in the stylesheet.
